## Cinema renderer

The corrected backend omits the panoramic OpenXR projection layer during
Far Far West's 2D cinema mode. Only the cinema/UI layers are submitted, so opaque
OpenXR composition supplies black outside the finite cinema screen at every
edge, independently of a scene texture clear or stale scene frame. The movie
quad is opaque too, including its black padding.

Movie eye crops now use the current source resource width and height rather than
dimensions cached during VR startup. This prevents sampling beyond a resized
resource during camera transitions. The changes are gated to this game's exact
executable and 2D screen mode; ordinary stereo submission is unchanged.

The recovery journal now keeps all five normal settings across menu/cinematic
transitions. The earlier journal could replace cinema keys with menu-only keys,
leaving screen mode saved on. Legacy journals are migrated.

## Unarmed dot

## Mount pitch and collision

The new mount module temporarily disables pawn-control camera pitch and inherited
boom pitch/roll. It keeps the game-camera orbit level, letting UEVR own head look
without a second inverted pitch/orbit contribution. Boom collision is enabled.
The accepted arm length, offsets, yaw, FOV and sprint effects remain unchanged.
Original properties restore on dismount, script reset or cutscene entry.
Actual terrain collision responses still depend on the game's collision setup;
automated tests do not prove the camera cannot clip in every terrain pose.
